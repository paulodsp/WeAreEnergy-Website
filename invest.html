How It Works

The Energy Future

This is not a product. It is a route to shared ownership in the energy transition. Members share their roofs and bill payments. In return, the co-operative delivers next-generation systems, maintains them for life, and generates additional revenues through a distributed virtual power plant, all of which flow back to members, not distant shareholders.

What Members Give

Their roofs

Use of their buildings' roofs for the system lifetime. The physical foundation of collective infrastructure.

Energy bill payments

Members pay their bills for the electricity they use. These contracted cashflows underpin the co-operative’s debt service.

Loyalty

After 8 years, members pay only for any electricity imported from the grid, mainly at off-peak rates. Generated power is free to use.

Democratic voice

One member, one vote. Members participate equally in shaping the co-operative’s future.

What the Co‑operative Gives

Zero upfront cost

Solar and battery systems supplied and installed - at no cost to the member or building owner.

50% off bills, guaranteed for 8 years

From day one, every member pays 50% below the Ofgem price cap. No lock-in surprises. No hidden terms.

Lifetime care

All maintenance, battery replacements and system upgrades for life. Free generated energy after 8 years. Our assets are owned and cared for by the co-operative.

Dividends & Virtual power plant

Our stable foundation of member bill payments is bolstered by growing revenues from our virtual power plant. Once sustainably profitable, these collective gains are shared back as annual dividends.

Four Phases to 2050

From 501 founding members to 11.5 million people experiencing energy democracy.

Phase 1 · 2026–28 · Proving energy democracy works

501 founding members. 120MW Tandem Perovskite solar factory in the North East. 91 direct jobs. First installations 2028.

Phase 2 · 2029–30 · Deepening community energy democracy

6,385 bill-paying members. Factory capacity expands to 1.2GW per year. Regional hub network begins. Wholesale partnerships launch.

Phase 3 · 2031–35 · National network

517,000+ members. 5GW annual manufacturing capacity. 4,600+ direct jobs. Virtual power plant fully operational. Recurring revenues across billing, export, grid services and wholesale building toward sustained profitability.

Phase 4 · 2036–50 · Regenerative Expansion

11.5 million people experiencing energy democracy. 52,000+ direct jobs. £265m annual member dividend pot. Carbon-negative manufacturing by 2045.

The Opportunity

Patient capital that serves people. No subsidy. Built to last.

We Are Energy brings large-scale manufacturing investment together with long-term contracted revenues, direct delivery of government policy objectives, and a co-operative structure that ensures capital serves public benefit. We are building the partnership base now. Our full plan and financial analysis are ready to share in a conversation.

Ethical and co-operative debt

Junior debt and co-operative lending instruments, sequenced to construction and scaling milestones. Contracted member revenues underpin the return from day one. We are looking for partners who help shape the instrument, not just provide the capital.

Impact-aligned investment

We welcome impact investors and mission-led institutions whose mandate includes measurable public benefit alongside financial return. Long-lived assets, contracted revenues, and a co-operative structure that keeps value with members.

Technology and innovation funding

Non-dilutive funding supporting technology development and commercial readiness, including Horizon Europe EIC. This is not infrastructure capital. It reduces risk for the partners who follow.

Patient senior debt

Long-tenor infrastructure finance secured against long-lived energy assets and contracted member revenues. The partnership we are building now is what makes this facility possible. Patient senior lenders are invited to begin that conversation.

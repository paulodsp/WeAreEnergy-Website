Who We Are

Who We Are

Our founder Paul, a qualified engineer and industrial designer, has used his skills to put customers needs at the heart of many well known organisations, including mobile phone networks; supermarkets; consumer electronics manufacturers and the NHS.

We believe that sustainable design can make the world a brighter and inclusive place, with better social, economic and environmental outcomes for all. And that innovation starts with the needs of those we serve and is the process of understanding how people, places, tools and services can be combined to create the best experience and outcomes for everyone involved.

Since 2013 we've been building knowledge and connections in the energy industry, convinced we could create a fairer and greener solution for the places where we live, work and play.
The result is We Are Energy Co-operative.

We start with people, not tools.

Inspiration

We Are Energy is building a member-owned movement that transforms how people power their lives through genuine energy democracy. At the heart of our mission stand the families, communities, and organisations whose lives we're changing - delivering immediate energy independence while creating genuine cooperative ownership of the systems that serve them.

We recognise that many home and business owners would like to install solar panels or heat pumps and make use of the latest technologies like battery storage. But fewer than 4% of households have the funds to cover the prohibitive upfront costs of £8,000-£15,000 for these types of systems. Meanwhile, energy poverty affects millions of families who remain trapped in a system designed for corporate profit rather than community benefit. So, we've launched a new co-operative with no upfront cost to overcome this barrier.

Making all rooftops co-operative

Mission

Our mission is to build a co-operative that serves to ensure its people, partners and customers can share in positive actions that help to protect our planet and deliver our vision.

We Are Energy is developing the UK’s first member-owned next generation solar gigafactory, demonstrating that when communities control essential infrastructure, everyone prospers. Our strategic implementation plan shows how democratic ownership can scale from 501 founding members to 10+ million cooperative owners by 2050.

We’ll do that initially by covering as many of the suitable roofs in the UK as possible, so that their surfaces can generate electricity using solar photovoltaic panels. We will develop tools and services to help our members use the energy they need in the most caring, creative and collaborative ways - helping to improve energy efficiency for everyone.

Trust, Regulation, Co-operation

Co-operative

We received our co-operative society regulated status from the UK's Financial Conduct Authority on the basis that we'll give an equal voice to all our members to help decide on our future. This includes our staff, our paying user members and consumer members - like their families - as well as our partners who help us to deliver the services we offer. Our membership is voluntary and open to all our stakeholders.

We are a business driven by values (caring, creative and collaborative) not by the payment of capital. We can't wait and sit back any longer for others to make things better for our children or soothe our conscience on the sofa of ethical consumerism. This is the decade to be active and intentional in our choices, to make our world a better place with clarity of purpose and positive action.

We received regulated status from the UK's Financial Conduct Authority under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014. Registration Number 4686.

We support the United Nations sustainable development goals, especially Goal 7 - ensuring access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern renewable energy for all.

The Cooperative Difference

Our cooperative structure ensures that all benefits flow directly to members rather than external shareholders, creating a regenerative economic model that grows stronger as it scales. This isn't just about clean energy - it's about transforming how communities can own and control the essential infrastructure that powers their lives.

Together we are inspired to face the earth systems collapse and acknowledge the impact of our actions on the sustainability of our planet.


Future Plans

Future Plans

In the future we plan to include food gardens as part of the installations for our customers, to help contribute to cleaning the air we breathe, storing carbon and helping lower the temperature.

And we are looking at ways to help customers collect power from wind turbines, heat water from solar collectors and generate heat for their buildings with heat pumps or hydrogen boilers.

Together with other committed organisations, we will help to build greener, smarter places to live: at home, at work and when we are out and about. A world we are proud to leave for the next generation.

We're already planning many exciting new ways to help the wider society - like donating energy to help prevent energy poverty, for example.

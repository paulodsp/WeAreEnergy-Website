Equipment & Installation

Superior, maintained and guaranteed equipment - installed in one or two days at no upfront cost to you.

We will install our superior, guaranteed and maintained equipment including our latest high-performance solar panels, an inverter, a smart meter panel and a battery. The battery can usually be fitted inside or outside your property - in a garage, or in your home in a cupboard - depending on suitability.

The inverter transforms the DC current from the solar panels into AC current for use in your property.

The smart meter panel shows how much electricity is being generated, how much is stored in your battery, and how much you are using. It also links to our app so you can see and control the system more easily, and links back to our systems so we can bill only for the energy you use and monitor performance. If something goes wrong, we’ll fix it remotely where possible, or arrange a visit as soon as we can at a convenient time.

Installing our system is a fairly simple process. From start to finish, it usually takes just one or two days to fit. Our local, trained and qualified installation team members will oversee the entire process, including the positioning and removal of scaffolding if it’s needed.

When you choose to join us as a customer, you will be required to sign a number of documents allowing us to contact your mortgage provider if you have one, secure access to your home and roof for our systems and set up a direct debit for your electricity payments. If you’re unsure about anything, we recommend speaking with a qualified solicitor.

Bills & Savings

50% off during the first 8 years. Free power thereafter.

You pay for the energy you use based on our tariff for the region you live in, and we only supply 100% renewable electricity.

For 8 years, our price promise means you will never pay more than 50% of the UK price cap for your region for your electricity. We’ll work out a flat monthly rate based on your current usage, so you know what to budget for once everything has been installed and your supply has switched over to us.

After this time you’ll pay nothing for the electricity generated by your own system and you’ll only pay for any extra electricity that we import for you. Most imported electricity will be delivered at off-peak times and you’ll only pay off-peak rates - on average about 40% less than peak rates.

Your electricity should cost you about 75% less than the average electricity bill for the rest of the 25 years the system is guaranteed to last.

After 10 years we calculate savings of over £8,800* and over £38,000* after 25 years - plus all the other benefits to the planet and your property.

You're in control

Your Property & the Service

You remain in control. If you sell your property, we will work with you and the incoming owner or occupier to keep the benefits of the system in place where possible.

Because the system remains part of our co-operative infrastructure, it is intended to stay with the building it was designed for.

You can sell your property at any time. Lower energy bills, sustainable living and an improved EPC rating can all be attractive to future buyers. There are several options available:

  • We can transfer the service to the new owner.
  • You can buy the equipment outright to add value to your property - it'll generate free electricity for the new owners.
  • For a small fee, we may be able to move the system to your new property so you can continue with us as a member.
  • We can also remove it for a smaller cost than buying it outright, depending on how long you have been a customer.

You'll have a signed agreement with us for the installation of the system at your building for this initial term. Should you wish to terminate the service early, we can remove the system from your property at a cost dependent on how long you've been a member with us. We'll contact you to let you know the cost.

The solar panels have a 25-year performance warranty, so you'll still be able to generate clean, renewable energy for many years to come. The electricity they generate is yours to use in your property at no cost from that point on.

Any extra electricity you need - during the darker months, for example - will mainly be imported from our renewable generation partners at off-peak times and charged at the discounted off-peak rate. This is normally at least 40% less than the average UK price.

The systems we install remain the property of the co-operative, but you retain the right to use the energy they generate for your own consumption whilst you remain at the property.

Equipment & Installation

Equipment & Installation

Superior, maintained and guaranteed equipment — installed in one or two days at no upfront cost to you.

We will install our superior, guaranteed and maintained equipment including our latest high-performance solar panels, an inverter, a smart meter panel and a battery. The battery can usually be fitted inside or outside your property — in a garage, or in your home in a cupboard — depending on suitability.

The inverter transforms the DC current from the solar panels into AC current for use in your property.

The smart meter panel shows how much electricity is being generated, how much is stored in your battery, and how much you are using. It also links to our app so you can see and control the system more easily, and links back to our systems so we can bill only for the energy you use and monitor performance. If something goes wrong, we’ll fix it remotely where possible, or arrange a visit as soon as we can at a convenient time.

Installing our system is a fairly simple process. From start to finish, it usually takes just one or two days to fit. Our local, trained and qualified installation team members will oversee the entire process, including the positioning and removal of scaffolding if it’s needed.

When you choose to join us as a customer, you will be required to sign a number of documents allowing us to contact your mortgage provider if you have one, secure a lease for your roof and set up a direct debit for your electricity payments. If you’re unsure about anything, we recommend speaking with a qualified solicitor.

Bills & Savings

Bills & Savings

50% off during your lease. Free power after it ends. Over £6,500 saved in the first 10 years.

You pay for the energy you use based on our tariff for the region you live in, and we only supply 100% renewable electricity.

During your lease period of 7 years and 8 months, our price promise means you will never pay more than 50% of the UK price cap for your region for your electricity. We’ll work out a flat monthly rate based on your current usage, so you know what to budget for once everything has been installed and your supply has switched over to us.

After your lease has finished, you’ll pay nothing for the electricity generated by your own system and you’ll only pay for any extra electricity that we import for you. Most imported electricity will be delivered at off-peak times and you’ll only pay off-peak rates — on average about 40% less than peak rates.

After your lease, your electricity should cost you about 75% less than the average electricity bill for the rest of the 25 years the system is guaranteed to last.

After 10 years we calculate savings of over £6,500* and over £20,000* after 25 years — plus all the other benefits to the planet and your property.

*Savings and costs are based on a comparison with the average latest Ofgem-quoted price cap for a London customer with no upfront contribution on a 7 year and 8 month lease, consuming 2,855 kWh electricity, assuming bills rise by inflation at 2% per year and consumption rises 2.8% per year.

Our price promise guarantee means you’ll never pay more than 50% of the UK price cap published by Ofgem for your region, updated four times a year. This applies for the full lease period of 7 years and 8 months.

We will provide you with a monthly bill which you’ll pay by direct debit. You’ll know what the bill is 10 days before the payment is taken.

The price will depend on how much of the lease has left to run. We will advise you once you contact us.

Your Property & the Lease

Your Property & the Lease

You remain in full control. Sell, move or stay — there’s a path that works for you.

You can sell your property at any time. Lower energy bills, sustainable living and an improved EPC rating can all be attractive to future buyers. There are several options available:

  • We can transfer the service to the new owner.
  • You can buy the equipment outright to add value to your property — it’ll generate free electricity for the new owners.
  • For a small fee, we may be able to move the system to your new property so you can continue the lease.
  • We can also remove it for a smaller cost than buying it outright, depending on how long you have been a customer.

There is a lease with us for the installation of the system. Should you wish to terminate the lease early, we can remove the system from your property at a cost dependent on how much of your lease has left to run.

The solar panels have a 25-year performance warranty, so you’ll still be able to generate clean, renewable energy for many years to come. The electricity they generate is yours to use in your property at no cost from that point on.

Any extra electricity you need — during the darker months, for example — will mainly be imported from our renewable generation partners at off-peak times and charged at the discounted end-of-lease off-peak rate. This is normally at least 40% less than the average UK price.

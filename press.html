How we work

Members share and pay. We own, make, install, maintain and run the system.

Members provide the roof space and pay their bills with no upfront cost. That gives the co-operative the platform to access patient capital to make, install, operate and grow the system.

In return, members get ownership through a one member, one vote structure, capped bills from day one, and the possibility of dividends once the co-operative is sustainably profitable.

This exchange is what makes the rest of our story possible: it is a co-operative model built to tackle energy poverty in a way the current market cannot.

Spokesperson

Paul Phillips

Founder, We Are Energy Co-operative

Paul is an engineer and industrial designer who has spent his career putting people's needs at the centre of products, services and systems. He began developing the thinking behind We Are Energy in 2013 after observing the structural barriers that keep clean energy inaccessible to the households that need it most. He founded We Are Energy Co-operative in 2020.

He speaks on energy poverty, co-operative economics, UK manufacturing, next-generation solar technology and the design of systems that serve people rather than extract from them. Paul is available for radio, television and podcast interviews.

We can also introduce members of the public who have registered their interest and are willing to speak about what the co-operative's model would mean for them.

Milestones

Now and into the future.

Timeline

Research 2013-2020

Paul Phillips began research into the structural barriers keeping clean energy inaccessible, building the foundations of what would become We Are Energy.

FCA registration 2020

We Are Energy Co-operative Limited becomes a registered society under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014. FCA Registration Number 4686.

Design and development 2020-2026

Strategic plan and financial model complete. Factory site identified. Manufacturing plans developed. Fund raising underway. Website launched and member registrations open. Councils and local authorities engaged on reserved-roofs partnerships.

Factory build and first installations 2026-2028

120MW Tandem Perovskite solar factory in the North East under licence from technology partners. 91 direct jobs. First member installations begin.

Community energy widens 2029-2030

Factory expands to 1.2GW/year. 148 direct jobs and 28 apprenticeships with 70 local hiring. Membership plans to grow to 6,300.

National network 2031-2035

400,000 bill-paying members and over 100,000 wholesale customers benefitting from next-generation solar. Made in the UK at our factory delivering 5GW/year. Our systems are built through ethical, slave-free supply chains. We expect to directly employ over 4,500 people across the UK.

Regenerative expansion 2036-2050

11.5 million people experiencing energy democracy through direct membership and co-operative replication. By 2050 we expect to be employing 52,000 people directly and many thousands indirectly across the UK.

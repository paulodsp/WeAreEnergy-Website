The barrier

People want solar and batteries for their buildings, but many cannot afford the upfront cost.

We Are Energy exists to remove that barrier.

The answer

Be part of the energy future

We Are Energy supplies, installs and maintains solar and battery systems at zero upfront cost. You pay for the electricity you use through the co-operative.

Register now

Zero upfront cost

Solar panels, battery storage and smart controls, supplied, installed and maintained by the co-operative.

Lower bills from day one

For the first 8 years, you pay half of what the Ofgem price cap for your region would charge for the same electricity, standing charge included.

Long-term care

We maintain the system for the long term, including battery and solar panel replacements as components require.

One member, one vote

Join the co-operative, help shape its future and be eligible for dividends once it is sustainably profitable, subject to the co-operative’s rules.

Be part of it

Every registration helps bring the energy future closer.

When people register, share We Are Energy and bring suitable roofs or buildings into the conversation, they help show where demand is and help build the case for the next stage of the co-operative.

After you register, you can share your referral link with friends, family and neighbours. If you have a suitable roof or building, you can also start a conversation about bringing it into the co-operative.

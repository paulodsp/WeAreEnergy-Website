Who We Are

We Are Energy

Our founder Paul is an engineer and industrial designer who has spent his career putting people’s needs at the heart of products, services and brands. That same design thinking shapes We Are Energy.

We believe innovation begins by understanding people's needs and then changing what can be changed in response: behaviours, services, places, systems and tools. Done well, sustainable design can create better social, economic and environmental outcomes, and help make our world more inclusive, abundant and just.

We start with people, not tools.

Inspiration

Many households and organisations want solar panels, battery storage, heat pumps and the latest technologies, but the upfront cost puts them out of reach. At the same time, millions remain trapped in energy poverty within a system that is built to extract value for shareholders rather than create lasting benefit for the people it serves.

That is why we launched We Are Energy as a co-operative with no upfront cost. Our model of ownership and value exchange is designed to make possible what the current system does not want to make happen: fairer access, shared benefit and genuine community power.

Making rooftops co-operative

Mission

Since 2013 we have been building knowledge, partnerships and practical ideas for a fairer energy future. The result is We Are Energy Co-operative, a business built to remove barriers, share value more fairly and show that clean energy infrastructure can be caring, creative and collaborative at scale.

We will do that by sharing as many suitable roofs in the UK as possible, so they can generate electricity using solar photovoltaic panels. With that, we'll develop tools and services that help members use energy in more caring, creative and collaborative ways, improving efficiency and opening the way to a more abundant future.

Co-operative & Community Benefits

We are a business driven by values - caring, creative and collaborative - not by the payment of capital. Our co-operative structure exists to give members a genuine voice in shaping the future, and to ensure that the value created by the business does not drain away to external shareholders.

This is not a normal shareholder business with green branding. It is a different ownership model, built on participation, mutual benefit and long-term stewardship.

We support the United Nations sustainable development goals, especially Goal 7 - ensuring access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern renewable energy for all.

Your roofs and money make it all possible

The Cooperative Difference

What makes We Are Energy different is not only what we install, but how the whole model works. Our members share their roofs with the co-operative and trade with it through their energy relationships and bill payments, creating the long-term value exchange that allows the co-operative to borrow, build assets, scale its factory ambitions and become sustainable over time.

The strength of our business comes from mutual commitment, not extraction. The assets are built to serve the co-operative and its members, and the benefits are designed to stay closer to the people who make the whole system possible.


Beyond Solar

We Are the Future

We believe abundant, affordable energy will do far more than change how electricity is generated. As it becomes more widely shared, it could reshape how we live, work and play, how we grow food, care for one another, make things and build stronger local communities.

It will not just change our buildings. It will change the way we do everyday things, and as those new possibilities open up, we will adapt our places, tools, services and the way we talk about them to support better ways of living.

That could mean bringing more fresh food production closer to home, exploring practical ideas such as installing green living borders around our solar panels to help clean the air and improve solar performance, and finding fairer, less extractive ways to store, share and use energy.

In much the same way that mobile communications unlocked new possibilities across society, we believe abundant, affordable energy can do the same once it is widely shared. But this time the value should stay with the people and communities that create it.

That is the future we are building towards. Join us, share us and help make it real.

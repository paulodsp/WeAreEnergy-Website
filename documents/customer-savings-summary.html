Customer savings summary

Summary of estimated savings and assumptions used to calculate the figures published on this site.

Key assumptions

  • Ofgem price cap reference used: latest published GB average value at time of calculation.
  • Household annual consumption estimate, generation profile and degradation.

Latest published assumptions

  • Source: Ofgem price cap data, updated 27 May 2026 for Q3 2026.
  • GB average Ofgem unit rate: 26.11p/kWh; standing charge: 57.19p/day.
  • Model basis: four-quarter weighted London baseline, 2,855 kWh/year, blended Ofgem rate 32.27p/kWh, WAE rate 16.14p/kWh, WAE standing charge 0p/day.
  • Published savings use 2% annual bill inflation and 2.8% annual consumption growth.
  • Published page figures: £8,900 saved over 10 years, £38,000 saved over 25 years, 25-year system guarantee.
  • Carbon savings applied: 16 tonnes CO₂e over 10 years and 22 tonnes over 25 years.
  • These estimates apply to Great Britain only; they do not apply in Northern Ireland.

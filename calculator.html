How these figures are calculated

Year 1 is calculated from the current Ofgem GB average price cap (Q4 2026: 26.32p/kWh, 54.83p/day) at our 50% price promise. 10 and 25 year figures scale from our published estimates of £8,800 saved over 10 years and £38,800 over 25 years for a member using 2,855 kWh/year, assuming 2% annual bill inflation and 2.8% consumption growth. Actual savings depend on your location, usage, roof and future Ofgem cap levels.

Who we are

The co-operative aiming to end energy poverty one roof at a time.

We Are Energy Co-operative is a registered co-operative society with a straightforward ambition: to make solar panels and battery storage available to every household and business in the UK at no upfront cost. Members pay for the electricity they use at 50% of the Ofgem price cap for their region for the first eight years. After that, the power generated on their own building is free to use.

We are currently in our founding phase - building the membership, the capital partnerships, and the infrastructure needed to begin installations. We are designed to be funded entirely by member bill payments, not government subsidy. As a registered co-operative society, we are legally bound to serve our members rather than external shareholders, with one member, one vote, and the prospect of dividends once the co-operative reaches sustainable surplus.

Our plan is to develop the UK's first citizen-owned next-generation solar manufacturing facility, in the North East of England, manufacturing panels using licensed Tandem Perovskite technology. The ambition, by 2050, is to bring energy democracy to 11.5 million people in the UK.

Five story angles

We Are Energy sits at the intersection of five significant news conversations.

Each has a genuine human dimension, real data, and a spokesperson available to support it.

Story 1

Energy Poverty - The Structural Problem

More than 6 million UK households are in fuel poverty. The barrier keeping clean energy out of reach is not technology or political will - it is the £7,000 – £15,000 upfront cost of a solar and battery system. We Are Energy's model removes that barrier entirely, with bills capped at 50% of the Ofgem price from day one. It is our unique response to a structural problem.

Angle: A co-operative aiming to end energy poverty one roof at a time - and why the current market cannot do it.

Story 2

UK Manufacturing - The North East Factory

We Are Energy's plan includes developing the UK's first citizen-owned next-generation solar manufacturing facility in the North East of England - a region with a long history in energy infrastructure and an urgent need for high-quality industrial employment. Subject to investment, 91 direct jobs by 2028, rising to over 52,000 by 2050, with 70% local hiring at each phase.

Angle: A citizen-owned solar factory for the North East - can co-operative capital do what private capital has not?

Story 3

Alternative Ownership - The Co-operative Model

In years when energy company profits reached record levels while millions struggled to heat their homes, We Are Energy chose a different structure. As a registered co-operative society under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, it is legally bound to serve its members. One member, one vote. Value that stays with the people who create it.

Angle: What if your energy company was owned by you? How co-operative law changes what an energy business can and must do.

Story 4

Next-Generation Technology - Made in Britain

We Are Energy's factory plan involves manufacturing Tandem Perovskite solar panels under licence - a next-generation cell technology delivering 30%+ efficiency compared with 20 – 22% for conventional silicon panels. The co-operative structure means the economic benefit of that technology flows to UK members and communities, not external shareholders.

Angle: Next-generation solar panels, made in Britain, owned by the people who use them.

Story 5

Policy Alignment - Warm Homes Without Subsidy

We Are Energy's model is designed to directly deliver two of the government's flagship energy policy objectives - the Warm Homes Plan and the Local Power Plan - without requiring government subsidy. Member bill payments fund the model. The co-operative's structure means it can reach social housing, housing associations and councils through reserved-roof partnerships, serving communities that commercial providers rarely prioritise.

Angle: A co-operative designed to deliver government energy policy at no cost to the taxpayer.

Spokesperson

Photo on request

Paul Phillips

Founder, We Are Energy Co-operative

Paul is an engineer and industrial designer who has spent his career putting people's needs at the centre of products, services and systems. He began developing the thinking behind We Are Energy in 2013 after observing the structural barriers that keep clean energy inaccessible to the households that need it most. He founded We Are Energy Co-operative in 2020.

He speaks from direct experience on energy poverty, co-operative economics, UK manufacturing, next-generation solar technology and the design of systems that serve people rather than extract from them. Paul is available for radio, television and podcast interviews, comfortable with both specialist energy and business programmes and consumer-facing features.

We can also introduce members of the public who have registered their interest and are willing to speak about what the co-operative's model would mean for them.

Milestones

Now and into the Future.

The research, registration, design & development, founding phases that led to the formation of We Are Energy, and our future plans.

Timeline

Research: 2013-2020

Paul Phillips begins research into the structural barriers keeping clean energy inaccessible - building knowledge; clearing regulatory permsissions with Ofgem and their legal teams; developing partnerships and knowledge with Elexon and others in the energy industry and establishing the foundations of what would become We Are Energy.

FCA registration: 2020

We Are Energy Co-operative Limited becomes a registered society under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014. FCA Registration Number 4686.

Design and Development: 2020-2026

Strategic plan and financial modelling complete. Factory site identified. Supply chains and manufacturing processes developed. Fund raising underway with paitent capital investors. Branding definition and design completed. Website launched and registrations open for new future members. Councils and local authorities engaged for reserved roofs programme.

Factory Build and first installations: 2026-2028

Proving energy democracy works. 120MW Tandem Perovskite solar factory in the North East, under licence from technology partners. 91 direct jobs. First member installations begin.

Deepening Community Energy Democracy: 2029-2030

Factory expands to 1.2GW/year to serve the growing membership. 148 driect jobs and 28 apprenterships with 70% local hiring. Membership plans to grow to 6,300.

National Network: 2031-2035

The full realisation of our cooperative vision: a member-owned energy system that serves people first. 400,000+ bill paying members transforming UK energy democracy and over 100,000 wholesale customers benefitting from next generation systems. Made in the UK at our factory now delivering 5GW/year ethical systems. We will directly employ over 4,500 people across the UK.

Regenerative Expansion: 2036-2050

11.5+ million people experiencing energy democracy through direct membership and cooperative replication, with 7 million bill paying members enjoying future energy and democratic dividends, whilst the remaining 5.4 million are indirect wholesale customers. And this only brings the UK's solar penetration to the same level that exists in Holland in 2025!by 2050 we expect to be employing 52,000 people directly and many thousands indirectly across the UK with our regional offices open close to where our customers live and work.

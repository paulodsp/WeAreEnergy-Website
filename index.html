The Energy Future

No upfront cost

Solar panels, battery storage and smart controls, supplied, installed and maintained by the co-operative. You pay nothing to get started.

Lower bills from day one

For the first 8 years, the electricity you use is priced at 50% below the Ofgem price cap for your region.

Long-term care

The systems remain part of our co-operative, we maintain them for the long term, including battery and solar panel replacements as components require, so they keep delivering value and supporting the wider system we are building together.

Free power generated on your building

After the first 8 years, the power generated on your building is free to use. Any extra electricity you need is imported separately, mainly at off-peak rates.

Co-operative benefits

You become a member of the co-operative and can help us shape its future with one member, one vote and the potential for dividends once we reach sustainable surplus.

UK co-operative solar factory

We are building a next generation solar factory in the UK. Our panels are designed, made and fitted by the co-operative with ethics, quality and community benefit at their core. Helping keep green jobs, skills and long-term value in our communities.

Councils, housing associations & developers

We work with councils, housing associations and developers to bring no upfront cost solar and battery systems to streets, estates and new developments. Contact us to arrange a conversation and explore how reserved roofs can help unlock delivery at scale.
